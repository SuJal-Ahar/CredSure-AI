# CREDITSURE INSTITUTIONAL CREDIT UNDERWRITING POLICY & GUIDELINES
Document ID: POL-CR-2026-V4
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applicability: Retail and Small Business Revolving Credit Facilities

================================================================================
1. PRUDENTIAL UNDERWRITING STANDARDS & THRESHOLDS
================================================================================
1.1 Minimum FICO-Equivalent Score Requirements:
- Tier 1 (Super Prime / Automated Approval): FICO Score >= 740. Full automated approval with preferred interest margin.
- Tier 2 (Prime / Standard Automated Approval): FICO Score 680 - 739. Automated approval granted provided DTI <= 40% and no 30+ day payment delinquency in the preceding 12 billing cycles.
- Tier 3 (Near-Prime / Conditional Review): FICO Score 620 - 679. Automated approval prohibited. Mandatory Senior Underwriter review required. Approval conditioned on minimum 6 months documented liquid reserves or primary co-signatory.
- Tier 4 (Subprime / Adverse Action): FICO Score 580 - 619. Presumptive decline unless supported by 100% cash-collateralized security deposit.
- Tier 5 (Deep Subprime / Automatic Decline): FICO Score < 580. Mandatory adverse action notice issued pursuant to FCRA regulations.

1.2 Credit Utilization Ceiling:
- Prudential Target: Revolving balance utilization (Statement Balance / Credit Limit) must not exceed 30.0%.
- Acceptable Ceiling: Utilization between 30.1% and 45.0% permitted for Prime borrowers.
- Hard Regulatory Ceiling: Utilization exceeding 45.0% mandates an automatic 50-point scorecard penalty and restricts credit limit increases.
- Overextended Status: Accounts with utilization >= 85.0% are classified as High Default Vulnerability and flagged for credit line curtailment.

1.3 Delinquency Escalation Rules:
- 30-Day Delinquency (PAY_0 = 1): Requires written customer reminder; credit limit expansion frozen for 6 months.
- 60-Day Delinquency (PAY_0 = 2): Immediate suspension of promotional interest rates; mandatory manual risk re-evaluation; automatic -70 points credit score deduction.
- 90-Day Delinquency (PAY_0 >= 3): Revolving charging privileges revoked; account transferred to Institutional Loss Mitigation & Collections.

1.4 Maximum Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratios:
- Standard Maximum DTI: 43.0% of gross verifiable monthly income.
- Exceptional Maximum DTI: 45.0% permitted only for applicants with FICO >= 720 and minimum 12 months liquid assets.
- Unconditional Decline: Any applicant with DTI exceeding 50.0% is disqualified from unsecured credit facilities.

================================================================================
2. BASEL III CAPITAL & EXPOSURE FRAMEWORK
================================================================================
2.1 Basel Expected Loss (EL) Formula:
Expected Loss is computed across all commitments as:
    EL = PD * EAD * LGD
Where:
- PD (Probability of Default): Derived from the empirical Weight of Evidence (WoE) Scorecard.
- EAD (Exposure at Default): Sum of current drawn balance plus Credit Conversion Factor (CCF) applied to undrawn credit:
    EAD = Drawn Balance + (20% * Undrawn Commitment)
- LGD (Loss Given Default): Baseline regulatory LGD benchmark is 45.0% for unsecured retail revolving facilities.

2.2 Macroeconomic Stress Testing:
Institutional capital reserves must withstand a 99.0% Value-at-Risk (VaR) 1-year loss horizon under simulated stress scenarios:
- Mild Recession Scenario: +1 month repayment delay shift across active borrowers, +10% drawn balance expansion, +20% default probability shift.
- Severe Stagflation Scenario: +2 months repayment delay shift, +15% balance expansion, +50% default probability shift, LGD elevated to 60.0%.

================================================================================
3. REGULATORY FAIR LENDING & FCRA ADVERSE ACTION COMPLIANCE
================================================================================
3.1 Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA - Regulation B):
- Non-Discrimination: Credit decisions must strictly rely on verifiable financial and behavioral metrics (payment history, credit utilization, debt load, income stability).
- Disparate Impact Prevention: Demographic attributes (Sex, Age, Marital Status) must not be utilized as discriminatory exclusion factors.

3.2 Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) Adverse Action Disclosures:
- When credit is denied or granted on less favorable terms, the applicant must receive an Adverse Action Notice within 30 calendar days.
- The notice must disclose the specific numerical credit score, the score range (300 to 850), and the top 4 adverse score factor codes (e.g., RC-01 Delinquent Status, RC-02 High Utilization, RC-03 Insufficient Payment-to-Balance Ratio, RC-04 Limited Credit History).
